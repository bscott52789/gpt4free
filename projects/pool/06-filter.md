# 06 — Sand filter and multiport valve

Photos: `filter-multiport-and-pump.jpg`, `filter-pressure-gauge.jpg`,
`multiport-clamp-warning.jpg`.

## What it is

Pentair top‑mount sand filter (tan tank) with a clamp‑style multiport valve. Water
enters above the sand bed, percolates down through ~20 inches of #20 silica sand,
collects in laterals at the bottom, and returns up the center standpipe. Dirt is caught
in the top few inches of sand.

## Pressure gauge

| Marker | Reading |
|---|---|
| Yellow START pointer | ~11 psi, the clean baseline set by the installer |
| Yellow CLEAN FILTER pointer | ~23 psi |
| 2026‑09‑08 | ~15 psi at 3200 rpm |

Rule: **backwash when pressure rises 8–10 psi above the clean baseline at the same
pump speed.** Pressure tracks pump speed, so a reading at 2200 rpm will look lower
than one at 3200 rpm on the same dirty sand. Record readings with the rpm.

Gauge reading 0 with the pump running means a dead gauge or a closed suction valve.
Gauge pinned high means a closed return‑side valve; stop the pump immediately.

## Multiport positions

| Position | Use |
|---|---|
| FILTER | Normal operation |
| BACKWASH | Reverse flow, lifts dirt out of the sand to WASTE |
| RINSE | Resets the sand bed after backwash, 20–30 s, to WASTE |
| WASTE | Bypasses the sand straight to the hose, for vacuuming algae or lowering water |
| RECIRCULATE | Bypasses the sand but returns to pool, for chemical circulation if the sand is fouled |
| CLOSED | Blocks all flow, **never run the pump in this position** |
| WINTER | Handle between positions to relieve the spring for storage |

**Never move the handle with the pump running.** The internal spider gasket will tear.

## Backwash procedure

1. Stop the pump. Note water level.
2. Press the handle down and rotate to BACKWASH.
3. Start the pump on high speed. Watch the sight glass. Run 2–3 minutes until clear.
4. Stop the pump. Rotate to RINSE. Start, run 20–30 seconds. Stop.
5. Rotate to FILTER. Start the pump. Bleed air. Record the new clean pressure.
6. Top off the pool. Recheck salt in a day or two; you dumped some.

Expect to backwash every 3–6 weeks in season. Backwashing too often keeps the sand too
clean and actually filters worse; a slightly dirty sand bed catches finer particles.

## Clamp warning

The band clamp holds the multiport valve to the tank under full system pressure. The
label's rule is absolute: stop the pump, wait until the gauge reads zero, then touch
the clamp. You only open it to service the valve or change sand.

## Sand replacement

Every 5–7 years, or when channeling shows up (pressure never rises, water never
clears). Use #20 silica pool filter sand or a glass media rated for the tank. Do not
use play sand. Protect the laterals when refilling by filling the tank half with water
first.
