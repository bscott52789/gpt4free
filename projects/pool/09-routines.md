# 09 — Routines

## Every visit to the pad (2 minutes)

- iChlor: FLOW light green, no WARNING light, glance at % and ppm.
- Pump: running, no unusual noise, no air pocket under the clear lid, no drips at
  the shaft seal (weep behind the basket housing).
- Filter gauge: read and compare to baseline at the same rpm.
- Heat pump: if running, warm air should be blowing out the top and the outlet pipe
  should feel warmer than the inlet.
- Look for wet gravel under any union or valve.

## Weekly

- Test FC and pH; dose acid as needed.
- Empty the skimmer basket(s) and the pump strainer basket.
- Skim, brush walls and the bench, brush the steps.
- Check water level: mid‑skimmer opening.
- Test the GFCI receptacle.

## Monthly

- Full test: TA, CH, CYA, salt.
- Inspect the iChlor plates for scale.
- Press TEST on the GFCI breaker.
- Check the heat pump coil for pine needles, cottonwood fluff, and grass clippings;
  hose it gently from the inside out if you can, otherwise from outside with low
  pressure. Never use a pressure washer.
- Compare the pump's Watts reading at each speed to the baseline.

## Every 3–6 weeks in season, or when the gauge says so

- Backwash and rinse (`06-filter.md`). Log the new clean pressure.

## Seasonal

See `12-seasonal.md`.

## Valve change procedure (heater on)

1. Open V2 (heater inlet).
2. Open V3 (heater outlet).
3. Close V1 (bypass) slowly.
4. Watch the filter gauge; it should rise a few psi as the heater adds restriction.
5. Set the pump to the heating speed, then power on the heat pump.

## Valve change procedure (heater off)

1. Power off the heat pump. Leave the pump running 5 minutes to carry heat out.
2. Open V1 (bypass).
3. Close V2, then V3.
