# 13 — Robotic cleaner: Aiper cordless

Photos: `robot-aiper-front.jpg`, `robot-aiper-top.jpg`.

## What it is

An Aiper cordless robotic cleaner, Scuba‑series styling (teal accent strip, rubber
tracks, twin top water outlets, charge port on the rear). Exact model is on the label
under the unit; record it in `maintenance-log.md`. It is battery powered, so it runs
untethered and returns to the wall edge when the battery runs low.

## How it fits the system

The robot is the primary floor cleaner. That lets the SuperFlo VS stay on its low
everyday speed instead of running high to vacuum. It does not replace:
- skimming pine needles off the surface (the skimmers and a leaf net do that),
- brushing the waterline and the bench,
- the sand filter, which handles fine particles the robot's basket passes.

## Vinyl liner notes

Rubber tracks are liner‑safe. Keep the tracks and brushes free of grit before each
drop so nothing abrasive rides under them. If the robot's brush ever shows exposed
metal or a cracked edge, stop using it until repaired.

## Operating routine

1. Charge fully before each run. Charging in the shade; the cabinet and battery do
   not like sitting in direct summer sun.
2. Drop it in with the pump running or off, either works. Give it 2 to 3 hours.
3. Lift out by the handle, never by the cable. Drain, pull the filter tray, hose it
   out, and let it dry open.
4. Store indoors or in the shade, off the ground, out of the rain.

## Battery care

- Lithium batteries last longest stored around half charge in a cool place. For the
  winter, charge to about 50–60%, unplug, and top it up once a month.
- Never leave it on the charger for weeks.
- Expect 2 to 4 seasons of full capacity, then a noticeable drop in runtime.

## Troubleshooting

| Symptom | Likely cause | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| Won't start or dies fast | Battery low or aging | Full charge; if runtime halves, battery replacement |
| Stops in one spot | Tracks jammed with needles or a stick | Clear the tracks and brush |
| Misses areas | Filter tray full, or low suction | Clean tray; check impeller for hair and pine needles |
| Won't climb walls (if the model climbs) | Low battery, slippery liner, or algae film | Charge; brush the walls; it is normal on a very smooth liner |
| Water in the charge port | Cap left open | Dry it fully, keep the cap seated when in the pool |
